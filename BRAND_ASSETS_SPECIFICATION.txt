🏛️ BRAND ASSETS SPECIFICATION (V2.0 PREMIUM)
VISUAL DNA FOR THE LAYUNIN EMPIRE

### 1. THE COLOR ARCHITECTURE
- **Primary Midnight:** `#050A18` (The Navy of Trust). Use for backgrounds and major headers.
- **Elite Royal Gold:** `#C5A02B` (The Gold of Achievement). Use for buttons, icons, and text highlights.
- **Success Emerald:** `#00A36C` (The Green of Growth). Use for success messages and "Active" status badges.
- **Protocol Grey:** `#475569` (The Grey of Precision). Use for body text and secondary labels.

### 2. TYPOGRAPHY STANDARDS
- **Display Typeface:** *Playfair Display* (Weight: 900). Use for H1, H2, and Display-1 through 4.
- **Body Typeface:** *Inter* (Weights: 400, 600, 800). Use for paragraphs and buttons.
- **Golden Ratio Scaling:** All font sizes follow the `--phi` (1.618) multiplier in the CSS.

### 3. IMAGERY PROTOCOL
- **Style:** "Luxury Productivity." High-contrast, clean desk setups, urban architecture, or professional portraits.
- **Resolution:** 
  - Featured Images: 1200x800px.
  - Product Covers: 600x600px.
  - Team Photos: 400x400px (Rounded 50%).
- **Treatment:** All images should have a 16px border-radius and a "Premium" shadow (applied automatically by the theme classes).

### 4. ICONOGRAPHY
- **Set:** FontAwesome 6 (Pro-grade).
- **Style:** Use "Solid" icons for navigation and "Light/Thin" icons for benefit lists to maintain an elite aesthetic.

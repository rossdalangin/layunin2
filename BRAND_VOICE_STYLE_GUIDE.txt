🏛️ BRAND VOICE & STYLE GUIDE (V2.0 PREMIUM)
THE LEXICON OF THE ELITE ARCHITECT

### 1. OUR CORE TONE: "EMPATHETIC AUTHORITY"
- **We are not:** Your "boss," a "guru," or a "hustler."
- **We are:** The Master Architects of Filipino potential.
- **Tone keywords:** Precise, Elite, Structural, Calm, Visionary, Localized.

### 2. THE LAYUNIN DICTIONARY
| Stop Using | Start Using | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Hustle" | "Execution" | Hustle is chaos; execution is architecture. |
| "Tips" | "Protocols" | Tips are cheap; protocols are systems. |
| "Users" | "Achievers" | We are building a community of high-performers. |
| "Cheap" | "Accessible" | We focus on value, not price. |
| "Try" | "Initiate" | Initiation implies a process has begun. |

### 3. SENTENCE ARCHITECTURE
- **The Short-Punch:** Use short, declarative sentences for authority.
- **The Local Hook:** Inject specific PH cultural nuances (e.g., "The Barkada effect," "BPO fatigue") to build trust.
- **Active Voice:** Never say "Results can be seen." Say "Architect the result."

### 4. CALL-TO-ACTION (CTA) PROTOCOL
CTAs should never be desperate. They should be invitations to a higher standard.
- **Low Intensity:** "Explore the Vault."
- **Medium Intensity:** "Initiate the 7-Day Protocol."
- **High Intensity:** "Apply for Mentorship."

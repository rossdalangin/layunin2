🏛️ CONTENT WRITING BLUEPRINT (V2.0 PREMIUM)
THE ARCHITECTURE OF A VIRAL PILLAR POST

### 1. THE RCT-ENGINE HOOK (DISPLAY HEADING)
- Must be a "Problem-Antidote" statement.
- **Example:** *"The Infrastructure Friction keeping Filipino VAs under ₱50k (And the 3-Step Protocol to Double Your Rate)."*

### 2. THE EMPATHY-BRIDGE (INTRO: 150 WORDS)
- State the "Friction" clearly. 
- "You are working 80 hours a week. You have the skills. But your systems are failing you."
- SEO: Use your primary keyword once in the first 50 words.

### 3. THE AUTOMATED OVERVIEW (TOC)
- The Layunin theme automatically creates this. Use precisely three H2 tags and two H3 tags per H2 to ensure an elite hierarchy.

### 4. THE MODULAR CORE (BODY)
- **Rule of 3-4:** No paragraph should exceed 4 lines.
- **Visual Breakers:** 
  - Use a `[benefit_list]` for outcomes.
  - Use a `[cta_box]` at the 50% mark for the Lead Magnet.
  - Use an AI-generated image (1200x800) with the "Elite" prompt style.

### 5. THE "LAYUNIN" CALL TO ACTION (CONCLUSION)
- Don't just summarize. **Initiate.**
- "Everything you just read is useless without execution. Click below to initiate your 7-Day Reset Protocol."

### 🏛️ THE PREMIUM CHECKLIST
- [ ] Primary keyword in URL slug.
- [ ] 3-5 Internal links to other Pillars or Shop items.
- [ ] "Table of Contents" title set to "Strategic Overview" in Customizer.
- [ ] High-contrast "Elite" featured image assigned.

🏛️ HIRING & DELEGATION GUIDE (V2.0 PREMIUM)
SCALING WITH ELITE REMOTE TALENT

### 1. THE DELEGATION AUDIT
Before hiring, you must identify your "Friction Zone."
- **Task Mapping:** Use the Revenue Tracker to identify tasks that earn <₱500/hr. These are your first candidates for delegation.
- **The Protocol:** Never hire to "Fix a mess." Hire to "Run a system." 
- **Rule:** If you can't write a 1-page protocol for a task, you aren't ready to hire for it.

### 2. THE "ELITE" CANDIDATE FILTER
- **Sourcing:** Use OnlineJobs.ph or LinkedIn.
- **The Filter:** In your job post, include a "Hidden Requirement" (e.g., "Start your application with the word 'Architect'"). This filters out 90% of applicants who don't read the details.
- **The Test:** Give a 30-minute paid test task. Measure **Accuracy** and **Initiative**, not just speed.

### 3. THE ONBOARDING PIPELINE
1. **Access:** Add them to your Slack/Discord.
2. **Architecture:** Grant "View-Only" access to the Blueprint Index.
3. **Alignment:** Set their first "12-Word Mission" for their specific role.

### 4. MANAGEMENT BY EXCEPTION
- Stop micromanaging. 
- **Protocol:** "I only need to know if the system fails or if a KPI is missed." 
- **Tool:** Use a shared Trello or Notion board for asynchronous velocity tracking.

*True scaling is the art of replacing yourself with systems and people.*

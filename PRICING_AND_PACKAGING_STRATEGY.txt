🏛️ PRICING & PACKAGING STRATEGY (V2.0 PREMIUM)
THE ARCHITECTURE OF PROFITABLE VALUE LADDERS

### 1. THE LAYUNIN PRICING FORMULA
**The Rule:** Price based on **Recovered Time** or **Future Revenue**, never on "Hours Worked."
- Formula: (Value of 1 Hour saved x Hours saved per month) x 2 = Your Price.

### 2. THE 3-TIER VALUE LADDER
**Tier 1: The Accelerator (₱1,499 - ₱4,999)**
- *Format:* Digital Guide, PDF Vault, or CSV Tracker.
- *Goal:* Immediate "Micro-Win" (e.g., Solving the "Prompt Engineering" friction).
- *Positioning:* Low friction, high volume.

**Tier 2: The Implementation (₱15,000 - ₱25,000)**
- *Format:* Group Mentorship or 4-week workshop.
- *Goal:* Guiding the user through the **Elite 7-Day Protocol** personally.
- *Positioning:* Results-oriented community.

**Tier 3: The Architect (₱50,000 - ₱150,000+)**
- *Format:* 1-on-1 Bespoke Systems Setup (Done-For-You).
- *Goal:* Total Structural Re-engineering of the client's business/life.
- *Positioning:* Scarcity and absolute authority.

### 3. THE "ELITE" PACKAGING SECRETS
- **Don't use "Course":** Use "Protocol," "Framework," or "System."
- **Focus on Friction:** Packaging should always be sold as the "antidote" to a specific PH-market pain point (e.g., "The Burnout Antidote").
- **Local Resonance:** Offer GCash/Maya installment plans for Tier 2 to increase accessibility for PH achievers.

🏛️ QUARTERLY SYSTEM AUDIT PROTOCOL (V2.0 PREMIUM)
THE 90-DAY CHECKLIST FOR BUSINESS ALIGNMENT

### 🗓️ THE AUDIT SCHEDULE
Date: First Sunday of every Quarter (January, April, July, October).

### 🔍 SECTION 1: THE REVENUE AUDIT
- [ ] What was the Total GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)?
- [ ] Which Level 2 asset had the highest conversion rate?
- [ ] What is the current Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)?
- **Action:** Kill any product with <5% conversion rate; scale any asset with >15% conversion.

### 🔍 SECTION 2: THE FRICTION AUDIT
- [ ] Review the "Distraction Shield" logs. What was the #1 recurring distraction?
- [ ] Is the current tech stack still the most efficient? (Check Perplexity AI for new alternatives).
- [ ] Identify one routine task to delegate or automate in the next 30 days.

### 🔍 SECTION 3: THE LAYUNIN ALIGNMENT
- [ ] Re-read your 12-word mission.
- [ ] Did your actions this quarter move you closer to this mission?
- [ ] Are you still the "Master Architect," or have you become a "Cog in the Machine" again?

### 🏛️ THE PIVOT PROTOCOL
If results are <80% of targets:
1. Audit the Offer (Is it solving a real PH-market friction?).
2. Audit the Traffic (Are the Meta Ads reaching the Overwhelmed Professional?).
3. Audit the Architecture (Is the site properly funneling users to the Shop?).

*If you don't audit the system, the system will eventually fail you.*

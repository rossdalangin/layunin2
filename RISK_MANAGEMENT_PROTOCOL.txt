🏛️ RISK MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL (V2.0 PREMIUM)
SECURITY & CONTINUITY FOR DIGITAL ASSETS

### 1. THE "ZERO-FAILURE" BACKUP PROTOCOL
- **Website:** Use UpdraftPlus or ManageWP to run automated weekly backups. 
- **Offline:** Once a month, download your full WordPress XML (Use our generator tool) and store it in an encrypted cloud vault (ProtonDrive/Mega).

### 2. ACCESS CONTROL
- **Password Hygiene:** 100% mandatory use of a Password Manager (Bitwarden/Dashlane).
- **2FA:** Mandatory Two-Factor Authentication on your Domain Provider, Hosting, and Stripe/PayPal accounts.
- **The "Hit by a Bus" File:** Keep a physical or digital "Emergency Protocol" that your business partner or heir can use to access core assets if you are unavailable.

### 3. LEGAL & COMPLIANCE (PH MARKET)
- **Privacy:** Your site includes a Privacy Policy. Update the "Data Controller" name to your legal entity.
- **Tax:** Consult a PH accountant about BIR registration for digital goods once you cross ₱250k annual revenue.

### 4. THE "BAD CLIENT" SHIELD
- **Screening:** Never initiate a Level 3 project without a signed Service Agreement and 50% upfront payment.
- **Termination:** Your Service Agreement must include a clear "Kill Clause" for scope creep or unprofessional behavior.

LAYUNIN SHORTCODE QUICK REFERENCE (V9.8)
DYNAMIC COMPONENTS FOR HIGH-CONVERSION PAGES

Insert these codes into any Page or Post using the "Shortcode" block to add elite functionality instantly.

1. CTA BOX (Standard)
Code: [cta_box title="Ready to Scale?" button_text="Get the Vault" button_url="/shop/"]
Usage: Use at the end of every blog post to drive traffic to your shop.

2. PRICING TABLE (Service-Focused)
Code: 
[pricing_table]
  [pricing_item title="Starter" price="P1,499" features="Audit|Map|Access" link="/contact/"]
  [pricing_item title="Mastery" price="P4,999" features="AI|Systems|Community" link="/contact/" featured="yes"]
[/pricing_table]
Usage: Pipe-separate (|) your features. Use featured="yes" for your most popular tier.

3. FAQ ACCORDION (With Schema.org)
Code:
[faq_page]
  [faq_item question="Is this only for Filipinos?"]Yes, the frameworks are built for our unique economic landscape.[/faq_item]
  [faq_item question="How long does it take?"]The Reset Protocol takes exactly 7 days.[/faq_item]
[/faq_page]
Usage: Crucial for SEO. Use at the bottom of sales pages.

4. BENEFIT GRID

  [benefit_item title="Clarity" icon="fas fa-bullseye"]Know exactly what to do daily.[/benefit_item]

5. TESTIMONIAL GRID (Manual Entry)
Code:

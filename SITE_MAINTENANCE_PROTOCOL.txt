🏛️ SITE MAINTENANCE PROTOCOL (V2.0 PREMIUM)
PERPETUAL PERFORMANCE & SECURITY GUIDELINES

### 1. PERFORMANCE HYGIENE
- **Image Optimization:** 100% of images must be passed through a compressor (ShortPixel/TinyPNG) before upload. Goal: All images <200kb.
- **Cache Protocol:** Ensure a caching plugin (WP Rocket/LiteSpeed) is active. Clear cache only after major Customizer updates.
- **Database:** Once a month, "Optimize Tables" via your hosting panel or a plugin to remove overhead.

### 2. SECURITY SCANNING
- **Updates:** Update WordPress Core, Themes, and Plugins every Monday morning.
- **Monitoring:** Use Wordfence or Sucuri (Free versions are enough for initiation) to monitor login attempts.
- **Login Shield:** Change your `/wp-admin` slug to a custom string to prevent 99% of brute-force attacks.

### 3. CONTENT FRESHNESS
- **Link Audit:** Once a quarter, use a "Broken Link Checker" to ensure all Affiliate Banners and Shop links are live.
- **Update Cycle:** Refresh the "Social Proof" section on the homepage every 6 months with your latest Wall of Impact stories.

### 4. CORE FILE PROTECTION
- **The Rule:** Never edit `functions.php` or `customizer.php` directly on the live site. Use a staging environment or the Local development tool first.

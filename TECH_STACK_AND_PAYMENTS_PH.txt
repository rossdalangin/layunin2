🏛️ TECH STACK & PH PAYMENTS (V2.0 PREMIUM)
THE PIPES THAT RUN THE EMPIRE

### 1. THE PAYMENT INFRASTRUCTURE (PH MANDATORY)
- **Primary:** GCash and Maya. Always include your QR codes in invoices.
- **Secondary:** Bank Transfer (BDO/BPI/Unionbank). Unionbank is best for tech-integration.
- **Global:** Stripe or PayPal (For international achievers). 

### 2. THE CONTENT ENGINE
- **Website:** WordPress + Layunin Theme (v9.8+).
- **Hosting:** Hostinger or SiteGround (SG Servers recommended for PH speed).
- **Email:** ConvertKit or MailerLite.

### 3. THE OPERATIONS VAULT
- **Project Mastery:** Notion (For internal blueprints) or Trello (For client velocity).
- **Meetings:** Zoom or Google Meet.
- **AI Workforce:** ChatGPT Plus (GPT-4o) + Perplexity AI.

### 4. THE AUTOMATION GLUE
- **Zapier:** To connect GCash (via email notifications) to your CRM and Course delivery.
- **Make.com:** For more complex architectural workflows.

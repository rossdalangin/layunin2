### 🧱 MODULE 1: THE MODULAR PROMPT ARCHITECTURE (MASTER TEMPLATE)
**The Layman's Terms:** Garbage in, garbage out. If you give a vague order to a genius, you get a vague result.
- **The R-C-T-C Formula:**
  - **Role:** Who is the AI? (e.g., "Act as a Senior Copywriter for a luxury brand.")
  - **Context:** Why are we doing this? (e.g., "We are selling a ₱5,000 productivity planner to Filipino CEOs.")
  - **Task:** What exactly do we need? (e.g., "Write 3 email hooks focusing on the fear of missing out.")
  - **Constraint:** What are the rules? (e.g., "Max 50 words each, use Taglish sparingly for resonance.")
- **Step-by-Step:** Copy this formula. Use it for every prompt. Watch the quality double instantly.

# 💎 ELITE TESTIMONIAL REQUEST PROTOCOL
## ARCHITECTING THE "WALL OF IMPACT" (V2.0 PREMIUM)

Testimonials are the "Social Proof Infrastructure" of your empire. Don't just ask for a "review"; architect a transformation story.

---

### 🏛️ THE "MASTER ARCHITECT" QUESTIONNAIRE
Send these 4 questions to your clients. They are designed to elicit a "Problem-Solution-Result" narrative.
1.  **The Friction:** "What was the #1 thing holding you back before you initiated our Protocol?"
2.  **The Transition:** "What was the 'Aha!' moment during our implementation?"
3.  **The Victory:** "What is one specific, measurable result you've achieved (Time saved, Revenue grown, System automated)?"
4.  **The Recommendation:** "If a peer was on the fence about joining the Layunin Circle, what would you tell them?"

---

### 📧 TEMPLATE 1: THE "POST-VICTORY" EMAIL (WARM)
**When to use:** 24 hours after a client hits a major milestone.
**Subject:** A quick celebration + a favor?

"Hey [Name],

I was just looking at your [Result] from this week—absolute world-class execution! 

I'm currently building our 'Wall of Impact' to show other Filipino achievers what's possible with the right architecture. Would you be open to sharing a 2-sentence blurb about your experience so far?

If yes, just reply with the answer to: **What is the #1 difference in your business since we started?**

Keep architecting,
[Your Name]"

---

### 📱 TEMPLATE 2: THE "SYSTEMS AUDIT" DM (CASUAL)
**When to use:** For beta testers or peers you helped for free.

"Hey [Name]! Hope the [System/Tool] I sent is working well for you. 

I’m trying to make the **Layunin Framework** as high-impact as possible. Could you give me some 'Real-Talk' feedback? Specifically, how much time do you think you'll save per week using this?

I’d love to feature your result as a case study!"

---

### 🎥 THE VIDEO TESTIMONIAL PROTOCOL (ELITE)
Video is 10X more persuasive than text. 
**Protocol:** Ask them to record a 60-second video on their phone (Portrait mode).
**The Script:**
1. "My name is [Name], and I am a [Profession]."
2. "Before Layunin, I was struggling with [Friction]."
3. "Now, I have architected a system that [Result]."
4. "The Protocol is a absolute game-changer."

---
## 🏛️ IMPLEMENTATION TIP
Always ask for their **Professional Headshot** and **LinkedIn/Website Link**. It adds 100% more authority to the testimonial on your site.

### ⚡ MODULE 1: THE ENERGY MAPPING SYSTEM
**The Layman's Terms:** Work with your body, not against it.
- **The Concept:** You have 3 types of energy hours:
  1.  **Green Hours (Peak):** High focus, high creativity.
  2.  **Yellow Hours (Mid):** Good for meetings, emails, and logic.
  3.  **Red Hours (Low):** Only for administrative and routine tasks.
- **Step-by-Step:**
  - Audit your energy for 3 days. When do you feel most "sharp"?
  - **The Law:** NEVER do Red Tasks (like checking Slack) during Green Hours.
- **Real Example:** 
  - *Architect Marco's Green Hours are 8 AM - 11 AM. He uses this time only for designing frameworks. He moves all client calls to his Yellow Hours (2 PM - 4 PM).*

### 🛡️ MODULE 2: THE DISTRACTION SHIELD (ENVIRONMENT DESIGN)
**The Layman's Terms:** If you can see a distraction, you will eventually use it.
- **The Protocol:** Build a physical and digital "Clean Room."
- **Physical Shield:**
  - Phone stays in a different room during Deep Work.
  - Desk has only three items: Laptop, Water, and your 12-word North Star.
- **Digital Shield:**
  - Use a "Cold Turkey" or "Freedom" app to block all non-work sites.
  - Disable ALL desktop notifications. Notifications are "micro-attacks" on your focus.
- **The Insight:** Every time you check a notification, it takes an average of 23 minutes to return to deep focus.

### 🗓️ MODULE 3: THE WEEKLY SPRINT PLANNING
**The Layman's Terms:** Don't start your week on Monday. Start it on Sunday.
- **The Step-by-Step:**
  1.  **Review:** Look at last week’s "Top 3" list. What didn't get done? Why?
  2.  **The Master Objective:** What is the ONE big win that would make next week a 10/10?
  3.  **Daily Triple:** Assign exactly 3 high-impact tasks to each day.
  4.  **Buffer Blocks:** Schedule 2 hours of "White Space" on Wednesday and Friday for emergencies.
- **Real Example:**
  - *Objective: Launch the Lead Magnet.*
  - *Monday: Finalize PDF copy.*
  - *Tuesday: Design landing page.*
  - *Wednesday: Setup email automation.*

### 🧠 MODULE 4: THE "BIOLOGICAL RESET"
**The Layman's Terms:** You are a human, not a CPU. Machines overheat; so do you.
- **The Pomodoro+ System:**
  - **50 Minutes Work / 10 Minutes Reset.**
  - **The Rule:** During the 10-minute reset, NO SCREENS. Look at a tree, stretch, or drink water.
- **The "Digital Sunset":** Stop all blue light (screens) 60 minutes before bed. This ensures your Green Hours tomorrow are actually green.
